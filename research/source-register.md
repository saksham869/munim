## Removed because no source supports them

These appeared in an earlier draft of the deck and were taken out on 23 Oct 2026:

- That Pi agents run "loyalty and cashback" for small businesses (S4 mentions only ads and WhatsApp leads).
- "Paytm's stated plan to charge subscription plus inference for merchant agents" (no source states any pricing).
- "Pi Inference" as an LLM endpoint.
- "Only Paytm has that network" (replaced by the sourced 1.57 crore figure).
- "Pi Agents or any LLM suggests an offer" (Pi suggests ad budgets, not offers; now "Any LLM today, Pi agents later").
- "Merchant-funded cashback ... the offer rails Pi Agents already uses" in a code comment (no source shows Pi using offer rails).
- Approval by saying "haan" to the Soundbox (voice commands are documented only for the AI Soundbox, S9, and a spoken yes is unsafe at a busy counter; approvals are now a tap on the screen or in the app).

## Labelled assumptions, not facts

- Business-model illustration: ₹99 a month and 1% uptake are assumptions to test, shown as "an illustration of scale, not a forecast".
- Gross margin of 12% for the synthetic kirana, the ₹500 weekly budget and the other policy numbers are demo settings chosen by the merchant persona, not market data.
